Your coding agent, on the go

TackRoom is the iPhone, iPad and Android companion to the TackRoom extension for Visual Studio Code. Your coding agent keeps running on your own computer; TackRoom lets you follow it and steer it when you step away from your desk: approve commands, answer its questions, read diffs and send the next prompt.

Every message between your phone and your computer is end-to-end encrypted. No account, no sign-up.
